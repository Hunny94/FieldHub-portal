Privacy Policy

FieldHub – Home Sampling Portal · Last updated 12 November 2026

FieldHub is an internal work portal used by the Home Sampling team and its supervisors to manage field work. This page explains what information the portal keeps and why.

What we keep

  • Your name, mobile number, employee ID, role, region and roster details.
  • Your recovery e-mail address (used only for password-reset codes and work notices about your own requests).
  • Work records you or your supervisors create: requests, tool issuance and acknowledgements, field-visit reports, expiry records, tasks and circulars.
  • Sign-in activity needed to keep your account secure, and the questions you type into the in-portal Assistant (kept for 14 days, then deleted automatically).
  • Basic technical information when something breaks (error message, page name, device type) and simple page-visit counts. These do not include your messages or personal content.

Why

Only to run the service: sign you in, route requests to the right people, send work notices, keep an audit trail of issued items, and keep the portal reliable.

Who can see it

What you can see and what others can see about you depends on your role. Supervisors see the people in their own regions; administrators see more. We do not sell or share data with advertisers.

Where it is stored

In a managed database provided by Supabase, protected by sign-in and row-level access rules. A weekly copy of the data is e-mailed to the Super Admin as a backup.

Your choices

  • You can ask for your account to be removed from My Profile → Request account deletion. The Super Admin will disable your login. Work records that other staff depend on (for example issued-tool history) may be kept for audit purposes.
  • To correct your details, ask your supervisor or an administrator.

Contact

Please contact your Super Admin through the portal or your supervisor.
